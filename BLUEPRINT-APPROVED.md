## 3. Positioning, key messages, tone

### 3.1 Positioning (APPROVED)
Medaglia Consulting is the specialist in creating, organising and developing sales networks for SMEs, especially B2B and industrial firms, in Nord Italia. It is a partner that works directly alongside the business owner and intervenes in the sales organisation.

It must **never** be presented as:
- a generic consultancy, or
- a recruitment / sales-staff selection agency.

### 3.2 Five key messages — must appear, verbatim or in substance (VERIFIED)
1. "Sviluppiamo reti commerciali più organizzate ed efficaci."
2. "Affianchiamo l'imprenditore, non ci limitiamo a consigliare."
3. "Ogni intervento è costruito sulle reali necessità dell'impresa."
4. "Diamo importanza a metodo, persone, attività e risultati."
5. "La collaborazione può partire dalla semplice consulenza e arrivare alla gestione operativa dello sviluppo commerciale."

### 3.3 Tone (VERIFIED + APPROVED)
- Professional, direct, plain Italian, understandable by owners who don't use technical terms. Explain or avoid jargon (KPI, pipeline).
- Form of address: "tu" throughout (D11).
- Conveys professionalism, competence, concreteness and closeness to the business owner. Not theoretical, not impersonal.
- Identity: authoritative, modern, concrete.
- No promises of guaranteed results. No undocumented numbers.
- "Misurabili" may describe the approach (objectives, KPIs, monitoring). Never imply specific results achieved.

**Definitive Interesse mapping (APPROVED, Checkpoint 2 final, option a).** The form exists only on /contatti/; the canonical of every /contatti/?interesse=… URL is /contatti/.

| /affiancamento-commerciale/ | /contatti/?interesse=affiancamento | `affiancamento` | Affiancamento continuativo |

| Any missing, empty, unknown or malformed value | — | — | Safe fallback: no pre-selection, no error message. Only the four exact values above are accepted; the parameter value is never written into the page. |

**Spam protection:** honeypot field. No CAPTCHA unless spam appears.
- Honeypot spec: off-screen wrapper with aria-hidden="true" on the wrapper (not on the input); the input has tabindex="-1" and autocomplete="off".

**After submit:** redirect to /grazie/ (noindex), which explains next steps.
- Who calls back and when: PRE-LAUNCH. Do not state a timeframe until confirmed.
- Whether the call is free, and its duration: OPTIONAL. Do not state until confirmed.

**Next to the form and every final CTA block:** a short note explaining what the discovery call is (VERIFIED purpose: understand the company's needs and assess how Medaglia Consulting can help).

### 5.5 Metrics

  - form_submit fires only on a successful submission, never on button click.
